The Session
AI is no longer a future consideration for finance professionals — it is already changing how teams research, write, review, advise, and make decisions. The deeper shift, and the one this session builds toward, is from AI that advises to AI that does the work: software that carries out multi-step tasks on its own. This 90-minute webinar shows finance practitioners what AI can actually do today — demonstrated live on real finance tasks, with clear guidance on the compliance boundary.

How It Works

Before We Meet — The Diagnostic and the Kit List
Ten minutes on a short questionnaire: your work and where the week goes; the tools and subscriptions you already pay for; what you’ve tried and where it stalled; the task you’d most like to hand over. From this I prepare your Build plan and a short kit list — the apps and accounts likely to matter, anything to install or update beforehand, and any overlapping subscriptions worth examining before we decide whether they still earn their place — so more of the Build can be spent building rather than signing up.
▸
The Build — Three Hours
▸
Connect the Working Core
Desktop apps, not browser tabs. Where your accounts and chosen tools allow, we connect the useful parts of your working life — mail, calendar, files — and turn voice input on, so you can talk to the machine faster than you type. Each connection made deliberately: what the tools can see, what they can’t, and why — because on your own machine, that judgement is yours to make and mine to inform.
▸
Run Your Day Through It
The everyday layer, built live on today’s actual inbox and calendar: triage, drafting by voice, meeting preparation, finding the thing you know is in a file somewhere. Small and repeatable — by the end of this beat, routines you can run again tomorrow without me.
▸
Hand Over Real Work
The centrepiece. One task you actually need done — a client-meeting pack, a research note, a proposal draft — briefed to a desktop-level AI agent and set running while we keep working. When it reports back we do the part that matters: what it got right, what it assumed, where it needs evidence, and which actions should always wait for your approval. Then we frame it as a workflow you’ll repeat, not a demonstration you watched.
▸
Set the Rhythm
How the pieces become a working day: what runs every morning, what runs weekly, what you never hand over, and how you check what comes back. Not maximum automation — a system whose decisions stay visible to you. The rhythm is the operating system; the tools are just its parts.

The Playbook — Your System in Writing
Issued within two working days of the Build. Where agreed, the session is transcribed solely to write the playbook; otherwise it’s built from working notes confirmed as we go. In it: your setup as built, with a reason for every connection; the prompts and briefs that worked; your daily and weekly rhythm; your verification points; and the next-build map.

Your Machine, Your Judgement
Unleashed does not mean unguarded. Owning the device and the accounts gives you wider choice; it does not remove your duties to clients, counterparties, or regulators — an adviser’s client confidentiality travels with you onto your own laptop. So we agree what material is suitable before we use it, and use sanitised versions where it isn’t. You authenticate everything yourself — passwords are never shared — and every connection is granted with a reason. Agentic work starts with a bounded scope and review points before anything is sent, changed, or published. Where a session is transcribed to write or update your playbook, that happens only by agreement, and the transcript is deleted once the playbook is delivered. There is no sponsor and no report to anyone: the freedom is yours, and so is the responsibility for what enters the system and what leaves it.
